# CloudMwalimu: 3-Minute Demo Script

**Goal:** show the full loop in one story, ending on the moment judges will remember (the Buddy's analogy in Kiswahili) and the readiness gap.
**Story:** "Amina has 12 days to her exam. Let's follow her."

## Pre-flight checklist (do this 30 minutes before)

- [ ] Logged in as the **seeded demo account** (realistic history: Storage and Governance weak, Cloud Concepts strong, about 40 answered).
- [ ] Browser zoom at 110 to 125%, notifications off, one tab open, dark mode **off** (light theme projects better), bookmarks bar hidden.
- [ ] Readiness is **not** "exam-ready" yet, and one domain is clearly short (for example "Raise Governance by 9% to unlock").
- [ ] About 10 flashcards due today.
- [ ] Study Buddy language set to English.
- [ ] Phone hotspot ready as a network backup.
- [ ] 2-minute recorded video open in another window as a fallback.
- [ ] Do a full dry run once. Refresh the page after the dry run so the data is clean.

## Script (target 3:00, practise to 2:50)

### 0:00 to 0:20 · The hook

**Say:** "I studied for AZ-900 and still didn't know if I was ready. So I built CloudMwalimu: one loop that tells you what to study, why you got it wrong, and when you're ready. This is Amina. She has 12 days."

**Show:** Dashboard with the gauge, domain bars and heatmap.
**Point at:** the "Next best action" card.

### 0:20 to 0:50 · Readiness and the plan

**Say:** "Her dashboard shows exactly where she stands: strong on cloud concepts, weak on Storage and Governance. And it doesn't just say 'not ready'. It says what's missing: raise Governance by 9% to unlock exam-ready."

**Click:** Planner. Show the generated day-by-day plan.
**Say:** "This plan was written from her real scores, her exam date and her 90 minutes a day. Today is Storage, then a short quiz, then flashcards."
**Click:** "Re-plan" or tick off one task to show interactivity.

### 0:50 to 1:35 · Quiz with real feedback (the heart of it)

**Click:** Quiz, Weak-areas mode. Answer one Storage question **wrong on purpose**.

**Say:** "Here's what most apps miss. She picked the wrong answer and she sees why it's wrong, why the right one is right, and a link to Microsoft Learn."

**Click:** "Explain it differently."

### 1:35 to 2:25 · The wow moment: Study Buddy

**Show:** Buddy opens with the question context.
**Type or tap a chip:** "What is an Availability Zone?"
**Say while it streams:** "Plain definition, then a Kenyan analogy, then how it appears in Azure."

**Read the analogy:** the M-Pesa agent shops on different streets.

**Click:** language toggle to **Kiswahili**. Ask the same question again.
**Say:** "Same concept, in Kiswahili, with the Azure terms kept in English because that's how the exam is written."

**Click:** "Quiz me on this." Show 3 fresh questions, labelled AI-generated.

### 2:25 to 2:45 · Flashcards close the loop

**Click:** Flashcards.
**Say:** "That wrong answer became a flashcard automatically. Spaced repetition brings it back until she gets it right."
**Do:** flip one card, tap "got it", show the box number move.

### 2:45 to 3:00 · Back to readiness and close

**Click:** Dashboard. The gauge or gap message has moved slightly.
**Say:** "Every answer updated her readiness, her plan and her flashcards. Five challenges, one loop. CloudMwalimu: know when you're ready."

## Rehearsal tips

- The most memorable 10 seconds are the Kiswahili toggle. Protect that time; cut the planner step if you run long.
- Don't narrate every click. Say one sentence, let the screen speak.
- If a spinner appears, keep talking about the idea; the cached demo data will keep the dashboard instant.
- End on the closing line. Stop talking.

## If something fails

| Failure | Recovery |
|---|---|
| Internet drops | Switch to the phone hotspot. If that fails, play the recorded video and narrate over it. |
| Gemini is slow or errors | Say "while that loads, here's how it's built" and move to the flashcards step. Show a previously saved Buddy chat in the history. |
| Login problem | Use a second pre-logged-in browser profile. |
| Wrong data on the dashboard | Refresh once. If still wrong, reseed the demo account (keep the command ready). |
| You blank on a line | Look at the screen and describe what Amina is seeing. |

## Recording the fallback video

1. Use the same demo account and the same script.
2. Record at 1080p, include voice-over, 2 minutes maximum.
3. Export as MP4 and keep a copy on your laptop **and** your phone.
4. Upload a link to the repo README.
